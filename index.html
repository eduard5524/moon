How It Works

From task description to shipped code in minutes.

01

Describe Your Task

Tell Moon what you need — fix a bug, add a feature, refactor code, or deploy a service.

02

Planning & Analysis

The Planner breaks down your task, analyzes the codebase, and creates an execution plan.

03

Autonomous Execution

Specialized agents execute each step — coding, testing, documenting — with full access to your dev environment.

04

Review & Deploy

Moon opens a PR with its changes, asks for approval when needed, and deploys on your command.
